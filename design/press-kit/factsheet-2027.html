The meeting

The CMCM Luxembourg Indoor Meeting is Luxembourg's leading international indoor athletics event. Organised by the Fédération Luxembourgeoise d'Athlétisme (FLA) at the Coque, the national sports and culture centre, it brings world-class athletes, Luxembourg's best and young talents together for one afternoon of competition. The meeting has held a World Athletics Indoor Tour Silver label since 2025, after Bronze from 2021 to 2024.
