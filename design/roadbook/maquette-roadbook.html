01 · Travel

Arrival & departure

Someone from the organisation will be waiting for you at the meeting point.

Plan de l'aéroport (image téléversée dans l'admin)

✈️From Findel Airport

  • Go to the tram station (public transport is free)
  • Tram T1 towards « Stadion / Lycée Bouneweg »
  • Get off at Kirchberg, Philharmonie / Mudam
  • Walk to the Meliá Luxembourg Hotel

🚊Tram hours

  • First tram to the airport: 4:00 AM from Stadion
  • First tram to Gare Centrale: 4:20 AM from Findel
  • Last tram at midnight
  • Airport ↔ hotel: about 18 minutes

✈️To Findel Airport

  • Tram T1 towards « Findel – Luxembourg Airport »
  • Get off at the terminus

🚆From / to the train station

  • Tram T1 towards « Findel » / « Luxexpo », get off at Philharmonie / Mudam
  • Back: tram T1 towards « Stadion », get off at « Gare Centrale »
